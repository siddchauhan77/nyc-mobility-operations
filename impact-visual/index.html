90-second answer

What did you build, and so what?

“I turned 3.7 million NYC taxi records into 436 evidence-linked investigation prompts. I built the quality layer, robust zone-hour baselines, five explainable detectors, evidence receipts, and a human review boundary. The system reduced alert overload by 94.8% and passed eight deterministic tests. The proposed buyer is a director of fleet operations, with dispatch managers reviewing prompts before shifts. I have not claimed fleet ROI. The next step is a 30-day controlled pilot measuring trips per driver-hour, revenue per online hour, empty miles, and pickup delay.”
